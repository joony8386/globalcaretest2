    How it works

    From request to visit

    1. Request a time

      Share your specialty, preferred dates, and which product families you want to review.

    2. We confirm logistics

      You’ll receive a confirmation with office location details and what to expect on the day.

    3. Hands-on session

      Walk through instruments and implants with our team—ask questions freely, no obligation.

    What to expect

    A focused, clinical evaluation

    • Time blocked for your visit—typically 45–90 minutes depending on scope
    • Product families staged based on your interest (trauma, spine, joints, sports medicine)
    • Room for colleagues or hospital stakeholders if you invite them
    • Follow-up materials or a commercial handoff only if you ask
